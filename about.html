About me

As an Electrical Engineering student at Florida Institute of Technology, I am developing my skills in electrical design, digital electronics, robotics, and automation while building a strong technical foundation for my future career. Engineering appeals to me because it combines creativity and problem solving to create practical solutions for real-world challenges. My growing interest in Revit MEP Electrical Design and Building Information Modeling has also introduced me to the important role engineers play in designing modern building systems and infrastructure.

What motivates me most is the opportunity to learn and continuously improve. I enjoy understanding how systems work, finding ways to improve them, and applying that knowledge to new ideas. Whether through coursework, independent learning, or hands-on projects, I look for opportunities to expand my technical knowledge and challenge myself as an engineering student.

Throughout my education and personal projects, I have developed experience with electrical design software, digital electronics, CAD tools, and engineering problem solving. My recent work includes Revit electrical design projects, a custom FPV drone project developed on a limited budget, and a window-cleaning robot currently in development. I have also completed LinkedIn Learning's Cert Prep: Revit for Electrical Design Professional course to strengthen my understanding of electrical design practices and industry workflows.

Outside of engineering, I serve as Treasurer of the Florida Tech Surf Club, where I have gained experience with budgeting, organization, teamwork, and leadership. These responsibilities have helped me strengthen the communication and collaboration skills that are important in both student organizations and professional engineering environments.

As I continue my education, my goal is to gain practical industry experience while expanding my knowledge of electrical design, automation, and emerging technologies. This portfolio documents my projects, technical development, leadership experience, and professional growth as I work toward a career in electrical engineering.
